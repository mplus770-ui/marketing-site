Decisions to make on this page

Hero hierarchy test

Both lines are strong. They are not interchangeable: one is a claim, the other is a description. A claim belongs in the H1.

Brand Promise as H1; the mechanism line sits beneath it as the supporting brand promise. The H1 creates tension, reframes the category and answers “why you” in six words. It is also the only one of the two that a competitor cannot copy without sounding like you.

Two directions at equal fidelity. The Zohar Glyph is not advanced, per your instruction. A search of the repository and its full 122-commit history found no existing ZOHAR Studio brown-and-gold asset — see the report accompanying this concept.

Known limitations of this concept

Project media is placeholder

Every showcase card is drawn in SVG and labelled as a placeholder. No external screenshot service is called. Real media awaits decision D12.

Fonts load from Google

Concept only, so the real typefaces can be judged. Production self-hosts WOFF2 subsets per §5.5 and makes zero third-party font requests.

Wordmark is live text

Rendered in Fraunces / Frank Ruhl Libre as HTML. Production ships it as outlined paths so it never depends on a font being present.

Aperture arcs are uniform

The expanding-spiral variation described in the proposal needs four separate drawn paths. This render uses one dash-patterned circle.

Only Home is rendered

Work, Services, Vision, About, Contact, Privacy and Terms are specified but not drawn. Case studies await D12.

Brief is step 1, disabled

Interface, validation and privacy wording are shown. The submit control is inert and no endpoint is configured, per your correction 5.

No tracking at all

Zero analytics scripts, zero pixels, zero placeholder IDs, zero third-party network requests beyond the font CDN.

Carousel JS is minimal

~1KB for arrow keys, prev/next and dot sync. Scrolling itself is native. Production adds the full ARIA and RTL key-mapping suite.

Green is judged on screen

The emerald system is specified for dark backgrounds only. Print and light-surface behaviour is out of scope for Phase 1–2 (D9).
