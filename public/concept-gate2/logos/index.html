Direction A Viable

A — Refined Aperture

Four straight blades form a square iris in a pinwheel. The opening at the centre is a diamond, not a lens, and one blade stops short so a beam escapes at the upper corner.

Static / reduced-motion
Meaning
The aperture is the instrument that admits light and controls it. The gap is the client’s intent entering; the beam is the presence going out; the four blades are the four pillars of the method.
Motion principle
The four blades close inward in sequence (4 × 90ms), the diamond opens, then the beam ignites. 540ms, once.
Strength
Structural and engineered rather than decorative. Solid filled blades survive 16px where thin arcs did not. The pinwheel offset is genuinely ownable.
Risk
At a glance it can read as a camera, settings or QR glyph. The diamond opening and the beam are what prevent that — soften either and it becomes an icon, not a mark.
Direction B Recommended

B — Light Fold / Zohar Beam

A narrow ray descends at an angle, folds at the vertical axis, and continues as two broad horizontal bars. The fold is marked by a luminous seam where ray meets structure.

Static / reduced-motion
Meaning
Light becoming structure. Business intent enters as a single directed ray; what leaves is built, level and permanent. Continuity is the unbroken path through the fold; memory is the second bar — what remains after the light has passed.
Motion principle
The ray draws from the source (380ms), the seam flares at the fold (140ms), then the two bars extend outward in sequence (320ms). 840ms, once. The same gesture as the hero at a different scale.
Strength
Non-botanical by construction. Legible at 16px as three solid shapes. Distinct from every mark in the competitive set, and it carries the brand sentence literally.
Risk
The two bars can read as text lines if the ray is weakened. The taper and the luminance of the ray must be preserved; the lower bar must stay offset so it never centres into a text-block.
Direction C Rejected

C — Seed of Light, de-botanized

Cannot be de-botanized honestly — not recommended for development.

The de-botanization attempt: every curve replaced with straight facets, the stem shortened so it no longer rises from a ground line, and the leaf silhouettes rebuilt as angular shards.

Meaning
Intended as a beam splitting into two facets rather than a plant putting out leaves.
Motion principle
Not developed. The direction did not survive review.
Strength
The facets are cleaner than the original curves, and the mark is legible at 44px.
Risk
The attempt failed. At every size, and most strongly at 16px and in monochrome, it still reads as a seedling. The botanical reading does not come from the curves — it comes from the gesture itself: two forms opening from a vertical axis is the sprout gesture, and no amount of straightening removes it. Pushing further would only turn it into Direction B by another route.
Recommendation. Direction B — Light Fold / Zohar Beam. It is the only one of the three that states the brand sentence in its construction, it is non-botanical by geometry rather than by correction, and it is the most legible of the three at 16px. Direction A is a genuine alternative and is stronger than its earlier ring form. Direction C is rejected on the evidence above.
