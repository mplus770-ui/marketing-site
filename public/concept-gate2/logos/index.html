New exploration

Three finalists

Seed of Light is retired. Light Fold is retired. Aperture is kept only as a reference and is not carried forward. These three are new work, built against the brief rather than refined out of it.

No recommendation. All three are presented at the same level of finish, deliberately. The choice is yours. The mark currently placed in the concept navigation is Threshold, used as a placeholder so the page is not headless — that placement is not a selection.

Ruled out by construction: leaves, sprouts, flowers, stairs, shelves, stacked bars, camera apertures, letter Z forms, sparkles, stars, suns, brains, chat bubbles, circuitry, infinity loops and religious iconography. Each mark is one sentence to explain.
Finalist

1 — The Cut

החיתוך

A solid block is opened by one precise incision. The two halves slide apart along the cut and light pours out of the gap. The shear is visible in the silhouette itself, so the mark is recognisable before any detail is read.

What it expresses
Radiance and controlled light: the light is not drawn on the form, it comes from inside it. Intent becoming presence: a single decisive act opens the material and something is revealed. Intelligence: the precision of the cut, not an icon of thinking. Continuity: one unbroken line crosses the entire mark. Premium: it behaves like a seal or a hallmark stamped into material.
Motion principle
The block is whole. The cut runs through it once (260ms), the halves slide apart (220ms), the light settles. 480ms, once.
At 16px
Strongest of the three at 16px: a solid shape with a bright diagonal band survives any reduction.
Monochrome
The seam becomes a true gap between two offset shapes. Nothing depends on colour.
Risk to manage
The diagonal must never sit at 45° or it reads as a “no entry” slash. The shallow angle and the offset are what keep it a cut rather than a strike-through.

2 — Threshold

המפתן

An architectural mass with a tall opening placed off-centre. Light comes through the opening and pools forward on the ground in front of it.

What it expresses
Radiance: a lit doorway is the oldest image of light revealed through an opening. Intent becoming presence: a threshold is the exact moment of crossing from one state into another. Intelligence: the opening is deliberately placed, not centred — a decision is visible. Continuity and accumulated knowledge: the mass is permanent; the light is what changes. Premium: architecture, not iconography.
Motion principle
The mass stands. The opening brightens from the top down (340ms), then the light reaches forward onto the ground (260ms). 600ms, once.
At 16px
Holds as an arch with a bright core. The pooled light drops below 20px and the mark still reads.
Monochrome
Mass plus void. The opening becomes the background, which is how the mark was constructed in the first place.
Risk to manage
It must stay a mass with an opening, never an outlined doorway, or it becomes a UI icon. The off-centre opening is doing most of the ownability.

3 — The Trace

הקו

One controlled beam travels around a square, gaining weight as it goes, and closes. It is thin where it begins and heaviest where it completes. The bright terminus marks the head of the beam.

What it expresses
Radiance: the whole form is made of light — there is no mass at all. Intent becoming presence: a complete, closed form created by a single continuous pass. Intelligence: the weight gain is controlled, not organic. Continuity and accumulated knowledge: the beam never lifts, and it is literally heavier for having travelled — the form carries what it has accumulated. Premium: restraint; one stroke, nothing else.
Motion principle
The beam draws from the terminus, around all four sides, thickening as it travels, and closes on itself. 720ms, once.
At 16px
A square outline with visible weight variation. Legible, though the taper flattens below 20px.
Monochrome
The variable weight is the entire identity and survives monochrome intact.
Risk to manage
The lightest of the three. It needs the weight ratio kept wide (1.5 to 3.7 units) or it collapses into a plain rounded-square outline, which is not ownable.
